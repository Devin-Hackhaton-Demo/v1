AI and data

Last updated: 19 September 2026 · Demo version 0.9 (fictional)

Fictional demo content. Coffeenator is a design preview. Every company name, address, registration number, tax number and contact detail on these pages is invented test data. Nothing here is a real company, a real legal document, or legal advice. Do not send personal data or payments to any address listed here.

Which model answers

Chat replies are produced by a large language model reached through a server-side proxy. The API key stays on the server and is never present in the browser. A message leaves the device only when the send action is used.

What is sent with a message

  • The visible conversation turns in the current tab.
  • Any context that is explicitly attached to the message.
  • No profile photo, no tracked subscription list, and no imported file that has not been attached.

Training

Content submitted through the demo is not used to train or fine-tune models, and would not be in the fictional live service either.

Limits of the assistant

  • Answers can be confidently wrong. Verify anything that matters.
  • The assistant is not a lawyer, doctor, accountant or financial adviser, and its output is not professional advice.
  • Status labels in the connector area are deliberately conservative: an authorised connection is not proof of a working credential, a health check or a completed sync.

Connectors

No live third-party account is connected in this build. Sample connection records shown in the app are fictional examples that demonstrate connected, healthy, expired and synced states. They are never stored as real connections and never enter the memory area.

Human oversight and reporting

A problematic answer could be reported to ai@coffeenator.example (non-routable demo address). In a live service, reports would be reviewed by a person and used to adjust prompts, guardrails and documentation.
