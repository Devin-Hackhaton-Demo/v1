What the demo actually stores

Preferences, profile name, theme, companion, avatar, tracked AI toolsStored in localStorage on the device. Never uploaded by this build.
Imported context text and notesStored in sessionStorage for the current tab only. Lost when the tab closes.
Chat messagesSent to the server-side proxy and forwarded to the model provider when the send action is used. Kept in the tab session for the transcript.
Technical request dataAn IP address is used transiently for rate limiting. This demo does not run analytics, advertising or third-party trackers.

Purposes and legal bases (as they would apply)

  • Providing the assistant — performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Security, abuse prevention and rate limiting — legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Optional connectors to third-party accounts — consent given per connector (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), withdrawable at any time.
  • Statutory records such as invoices — legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Retention (fictional schedule)

  • Local preferences: until cleared from the device.
  • Imported text: until the browser tab is closed.
  • Chat transcripts on the server: not retained by this demo beyond the request.
  • Security logs: 30 days. Accounting records: 8 years.

Recipients and transfers

In a live service, processors would include the hosting, database and model providers listed in the legal notice. Transfers outside the EEA would rely on the EU standard contractual clauses plus supplementary measures. No data is sold, and no data is shared with advertisers.

Automated decision-making

The assistant generates text suggestions. It makes no automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects, and its output should always be reviewed by a person.
